What each merge run did to the program, in order, and what the program's dialogs showed.
Condensed from the step logs: the presses, the keys, the files written over, and the title, message
and buttons of every dialog met. A resource identifier stands where a control had no text. In the
KeePassDX message `<time>, <size>` stands for a local time and a file size, and `->` for an arrow.
A `typed` line gives the field as the flow selected it and then the text. A `file` line is the
target as the device listed it: its bytes at the first listing, and after that `unchanged` where
neither its bytes nor its time had moved since the listing before and `rewritten` where either had.
`flow ended 0` is a run that went through. Runs of a cell that did the same are given once.
